
Artificial intelligence (AI) is intelligence demonstrated by machines,
as opposed to the natural intelligence displayed by humans and other animals.

Leading AI textbooks define the field as the study of intelligent agents:
systems that perceive their environment and take actions that maximize
the achievement of goals.

Some high-profile applications of AI include advanced web search engines,
recommendation systems, interacting via human speech, autonomous vehicles,
generative and creative tools, and superhuman play and analysis in strategy
games.

Artificial intelligence was founded as an academic discipline in 1956.
The field went through multiple cycles of optimism, followed by periods
of disappointment and loss of funding, known as AI winters.

Machine learning is an important part of modern artificial intelligence.
Instead of explicitly programming every rule, machine learning systems
learn patterns from data. These systems can then use the learned patterns
to make predictions or decisions when presented with new data.

Deep learning is a type of machine learning based on artificial neural
networks with multiple layers. Deep learning has achieved significant
success in areas such as computer vision, speech recognition, natural
language processing, and game playing.

Natural language processing allows computers to process, understand,
generate, and communicate using human language. It is used in applications
such as translation, chatbots, text classification, information retrieval,
and speech assistants.

Computer vision is another major area of artificial intelligence.
It enables computers to interpret information from images and videos.
Applications include facial recognition, medical image analysis,
autonomous driving, and object detection.

Artificial intelligence can be divided into different categories.
Artificial narrow intelligence refers to systems designed to perform
specific tasks. Artificial general intelligence refers to a hypothetical
machine capable of understanding and performing a wide range of intellectual
tasks at a level comparable to humans.

AI systems can provide significant benefits, including automation,
improved decision-making, faster analysis of large amounts of information,
and assistance in scientific research.

However, artificial intelligence also creates challenges. These include
privacy concerns, bias in automated decisions, security risks,
job displacement, misinformation, and questions about accountability.

Researchers continue to study how to make AI systems more reliable,
transparent, safe, and useful. The long-term development of artificial
intelligence remains an active area of research.
